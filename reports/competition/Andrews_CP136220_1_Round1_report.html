Quick glossary (first-use Capsim terms): An emergency loan is automatic short-term borrowing Capsim forces when cash would otherwise go negative — expensive, and a signal that financing did not match spending. Contribution margin (CM) is the percent of each sales dollar left after variable production costs (material, labor, inventory carrying) — before SG&A, depreciation, and interest. Awareness is the share of customers who know your product exists (mainly promotion budget). Accessibility is how easy it is for customers to find and buy you in the sales channel (mainly sales budget, shared across products). A fine cut is the “sweet spot” on the perceptual map for a segment; customers strongly prefer products inside it. MTBF (Mean Time Between Failure) is reliability in hours; each segment has a required band. SG&A is selling, general & administrative expense (promotion, sales, admin).

The hopeful read: emergency loans are a loud teacher. Products with large unsold stacks: Able (285 units), Adam (251 units). Unsold units are cash sitting on a shelf. Align next round’s production with realistic demand.
  3. 3. Able needs a reliability lift. MTBF is 13,500 hours — below the Traditional band of 14,000–19,000. Raise reliability into (ideally toward the middle of) the band.
  4. 4. Aft lost money on every unit after variable costs. Contribution margin was -181.7% with 17 sold and 504 in inventory. Reset price, cost, and segment fit before producing more.
  5. 5. Aft’s price did not match its segment. Aft sits in Performance territory but was priced at $39.50, while that segment’s band is about $24.50–$34.50. Round 2 fix: price inside the band for the segment you are in, or reposition toward a segment where that price can work. Do not schedule another large run until the match is coherent.
  6. 6. You stocked out where customers wanted more — a good problem, if you respond. Stockouts on Acre mean demand exceeded supply. Raise production (and check capacity), and fund accessibility so you do not leave sales on the table.
  7. 7. Margin and overhead need a closer handshake. Company CM 26.2% and SG&A-to-sales 21.2%. Pair marketing budgets with product fixes — not instead of them.
The through-line: specific product and cash-planning choices created a shortfall Capsim filled with an emergency loan. Round 2 is absolutely recoverable — fix product economics, match production to demand, and arrange financing before cash hits zero.
